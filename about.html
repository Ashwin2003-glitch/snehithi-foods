SNEHITHI Home grown foods was born from a simple passion — to bring wholesome, authentic, homestyle traditional foods, nutritious ladoos, pure ghee, and flavorful thokkus to every dining table, crafted just like grandmother's cherished recipes.

Every single batch is lovingly homemade using pure traditional ingredients, native millets, authentic cow ghee, natural jaggery, and selected spices — with zero artificial preservatives, zero synthetic colors, and zero compromise on hygiene and taste.
